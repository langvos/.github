# Langvos Labs

### Language. Reasoning. Representation. And Building.

Langvos explores language, reasoning and representation through computational linguistics. We connect original research with practical engineering to turn theory into applications and solutions that address real needs.

![A landscape of language: multilingual words across a flowing wireframe surface.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/langvos/.github/main/profile/assets/langvos.png)

## Our mission

Advance the understanding of language, reasoning and representation through original computational research, and transform that knowledge into practical methods, tools and solutions.

## Our vision

Build a research environment where ambitious ideas about language become rigorous investigations and useful technologies—connecting scientific curiosity with real-world impact.

## Research directions

- **Language and meaning:** how words, context and linguistic structure contribute to understanding.
- **Reasoning and representation:** how models organize knowledge and support inference.
- **Learning from linguistic evidence:** what can be learned from a corpus under explicit information constraints.
- **Evaluation and applied systems:** benchmarks, experimental workflows and software that connect research to practical applications.

## From evidence to models

**Understanding the world through evidence, data and behavior.**

Langvos aims to build models that use language to represent the world, reason about it and turn understanding into practical solutions.

## The Popkorn project

Which words matter most for understanding a sentence?

Popkorn investigates how to rank word occurrences by their contribution to sentence understanding in an unknown language, using only a corpus in that language. The project brings together corpus-based estimation, benchmark construction and human judgments to examine this question experimentally.

[Explore Popkorn →](https://labs.langvos.org/popkorn)
